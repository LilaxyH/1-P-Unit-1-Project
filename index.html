Frankenstein (1818 edition)

By Marry Wolstoncraft Shelly

Date started: September 20th, 2026

Date finished: hopefully sometime soon

Initial Thoughts:

I started this book because I've been wanting to read more, and it seemed like a good fall/halloween read. I also heard the recent movie adapation was good, so I wanted to read the original before I watched it. This version caught my eye becuase it is the original, unedited verison of the book, which was published almost 10 years before the most well known edition. According to the back cover and introduction, this version of Frankenstein (the original) was published anonymously and did not sell well. It was only after it was adapted into a popular stage play and a third, and heavily edited, edition was published in 1832 that the book became a commerical success.
